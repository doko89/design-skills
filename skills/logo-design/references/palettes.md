## Tips
- Gradient direction: usually top-left to bottom-right (`x1=0 y1=0 x2=1 y2=1`). Keep the same direction across all shapes in the mark so lighting feels consistent.
- For depth, give each overlapping layer a slightly different stop (lighter on top or darker under).
- Always check the mark on white AND on #0B1020. If it fails on dark, supply a reversed or mono version.
- Mono version: single color `#111827` on light, `#FFFFFF` on dark; keep the same shapes and use the same cut-outs.
