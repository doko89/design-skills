# Logo concept library

Pick concepts that fit the brand, then execute one idea cleanly. These are the families
seen in contemporary gradient-geometric logo work.

## 1. Letter monogram from simple shapes
Build the initial from 2-3 bold primitives (rounded rectangles, quarter circles, ribbons).
- M / N / H: two diagonal ribbons crossing, or two rounded pillars joined by a diagonal
- S: two offset C-shapes or a folded ribbon (one shape passes over the other)
- D: a rounded half-circle with stacked "speed" slices cut into the left edge (stripe motion)
- Y: three rounded arms meeting in a circle/hex, or a three-lobe knot
- C: a thick open ring with an offset inner circle, or two overlapping crescents
- A: triangle built from two ribbons and a notch of negative space

## 2. Overlapping translucent shapes
Two or three identical shapes offset along a diagonal, each with a slightly different
gradient/opacity. Reads as layers, documents, stacks, progress. (cards, folded paper,
stacked chevrons, `>>` arrows for speed/forward motion)

## 3. Negative-space icon inside a container
A solid shape (hexagon, rounded triangle, circle, squircle) with a small icon cut out:
drop, bolt, arrow, circle, leaf, keyhole, play-triangle. Cut with `<mask>` or evenodd.

## 4. Ribbon / infinity / loop
One continuous band that folds or twists (figure-8, knot, Moebius-like). Draw as a thick
stroke with round caps, then fake the over/under crossing by redrawing a short segment on top.
Good for: connection, flow, finance, logistics, "infinite" ideas.

## 5. Rotational / radial symmetry
Petals, clover, starburst, snowflake, flower from 3-8 identical rotated elements
(`transform="rotate(angle 256 256)"`). Good for: community, wellness, platforms.

## 6. Isometric cube / hexagon
Three rhombuses in 3 tints of one hue = a cube. Good for: infrastructure, storage, security,
blocks, supply chain.

## 7. Dots and pill grid
Rows of rounded pills / circles in varied colors forming a letter or a pattern
(e.g. a "T" from pills). Good for: playful, apps, productivity, kids.

## 8. Fruit / drop / flame / leaf organic shape
One smooth silhouette with a spiral or inner highlight and a warm gradient (orange to red to
yellow). Good for: food, energy, wellness, eco.

## 9. Compass / planet / orbit
A circle or ring with a tilted second ring passing in front and behind (use clipPath to break
the ring). Good for: space, global, data, search.

## 10. Wordmark with one custom glyph
Short brand name in clean sans-serif, with one letter replaced by a geometric symbol
(o becomes a circle mark, i gets a colored dot). Good for: brands that want text-first.

## Anti-patterns (avoid)
- Literal clip-art (globe + handshake, lightbulb + gear)
- More than 3 colors, rainbow gradients without a reason
- Thin strokes, tiny gaps (they disappear at favicon size)
- Drop shadows, bevels, glossy 2000s effects
- Mixing many unrelated shapes: pick one idea
